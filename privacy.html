Thai Flash Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Thai Flash does not collect, store or share any personal data.

  • Your progress, settings and imported decks are stored only on your device.
  • The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and makes no network requests.
  • Daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • Pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voice.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data. You can also clear your progress in Settings → Reset progress.
